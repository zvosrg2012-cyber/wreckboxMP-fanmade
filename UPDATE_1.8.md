# Fan update 1.8 — transformer shed

- Replaced visible colored wires and pole overlays with a physical, destructible transformer shed.
- The shed is placed using the generated building layout and local terrain, not hard-coded map coordinates.
- The transformer connects a generated group of 2–4 buildings; no connection lines are rendered.
- Damaging the shed's structural cells cuts power to the connected houses and switches off their associated lights.
- The shed is built from the game's normal destructible material cells, so demolition tools can break it.
